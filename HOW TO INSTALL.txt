EditFlow Pro — Installation Guide
===================================

STEP 1 — Open the DMG
Double-click "EditFlow Pro Installer.dmg" to mount it.

STEP 2 — Run the Installer
Right-click "Install EditFlow Pro" → click Open → click Open again.
(This one-time step is required on macOS for unnotarized apps.)

STEP 3 — Wait for the success message
The installer will automatically:
  • Copy the extension to Adobe Premiere Pro
  • Enable the extension for your version of Premiere

STEP 4 — Restart Premiere Pro
Close and reopen Adobe Premiere Pro.

STEP 5 — Open the panel
Go to:  Window → Extensions → EditFlow Pro

That's it — you're ready to go.

---

FIRST-TIME AI CAPTIONS
On the first use of AI Captions, the tool will automatically
download a small AI model (~460 MB) in the background.
Make sure you're connected to the internet for this step.

---

TROUBLESHOOTING
• Extension not appearing? Make sure you restarted Premiere Pro after installing.
• "Panel failed to load"? Try: Window → Extensions → EditFlow Pro again.
