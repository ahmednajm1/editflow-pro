# EditFlow Pro - Business & Marketing Strategy

## 1. Product Positioning
**Target Audience:** Freelance video editors, YouTube creators, digital marketing agencies, and podcast editors.
**Slogan:** "Stop clicking. Start creating. Cut your Premiere Pro workflow by 50%."

Many professional editors spend hours on robotic tasks (cutting silence, ducking audio, renaming batches). EditFlow Pro removes the grunt work completely using smart AI analysis and workflow automation directly inside Premiere Pro.

## 2. Competitive Advantage
- **Zero Cloud Reliance:** All DSP (Digital Signal Processing) for silence and beat detection happens locally. No uploading gigabytes of proxies.
- **Native Vibe:** The panel exactly mimics Premiere Pro's native properties window, making it feel like a built-in Adobe solution.
- **Non-destructive:** We rely on markers and standard clip edits via the ExtendScript API. If the user uninstalls the plugin, their sequence remains perfectly intact. No proprietary custom effects.

## 3. Pricing Strategy

### Tier 1: Free (Lead Magnet)
*   Access to the panel.
*   1-Click Dialogue Normalizer (-6dB target).
*   *Purpose:* Gets users to install the plugin and see the UI. The trial badge creates FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) for the advanced tools.

### Tier 2: Pro
*   **Price:** $15/month or $149 Lifetime.
*   **Features:** Single Sequence Smart Export, Basic Silence Removal, Auto-Ducking.
*   *Value:* Saving an editor 1 hour per week easily justifies a $149 lifetime price.

### Tier 3: Studio
*   **Price:** $29/month or $249 Lifetime.
*   **Features:** Batch Exporting (for short-form content agencies), Advanced Beat Detection (music syncing), and priority updates.
*   *Value:* Built for TikTok/Reels/Shorts agencies churning out hundreds of clips daily. Batch export alone pays for the plugin in a week.

## 4. Go-to-Market Strategy
- **Distribution:** Sell via Gumroad or LemonSqueezy (using their API for license validation in the Node.js backend).
- **Adobe Exchange:** List the Free tier on the Adobe Exchange Marketplace as a lead generation funnel.
- **Affiliate Marketing:** Offer a 30% commission to YouTube Premiere Pro tutorial channels (e.g., Premiere Gal, Finzar, Justin Odisho).
